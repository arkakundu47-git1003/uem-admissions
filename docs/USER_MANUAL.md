# User manual

Portal address: https://uem-admissions-ten.vercel.app. The first page load after a quiet period can take up to a minute while the server wakes; the page tells you so. Please wait.

## Applicants

**Register and sign in.** Choose Register, enter name, email, phone, date of birth and a password that meets the rules shown. Sign in with your email and password. If you forget the password, use "Forgot password" and follow the emailed link.

**Choose a course.** Browse Courses without signing in. Open a course to see eligibility, seats and fee, then choose Apply.

**Fill in the application.** The form has several steps. Your work is saved as a draft; you can leave and return from your dashboard.

**Upload documents.** Upload each required document as PDF, JPG or PNG within the size limit shown. Files of the wrong type are refused. You can remove and replace a file before submission.

**Pay the fee.** If the course has an application fee, choose a method (UPI, card or net banking). In the current version payment is a **simulation** and no money is taken.

**Submit.** Submit is refused until the details, required documents and any fee are complete. After submission you cannot edit the application, except to replace a document an officer has rejected.

**Track progress.** Your dashboard shows the status: submitted, under review, documents verified, accepted, waitlisted or rejected. Open the application for its history and any remarks. Notifications appear under the bell and may also be emailed.

## Admission officers

Sign in with the account an administrator created for you. **Applications** lists the review queue. Open one to view details and documents; verify each document or reject it with a remark. Then move the status forward: under review, documents verified, then accepted, waitlisted or rejected. Rejection and waitlisting need a remark. Acceptance needs every required document verified and a free seat. Every change notifies the applicant. Officers can export CSV reports.

## Coordinators

Same screens as officers, limited to your own department's applications and figures.

## Administrators

- **Courses:** create, edit, close or delete a course (delete is refused if it has applications).
- **Users:** create officer, coordinator or admin accounts (coordinators need a department), search, deactivate or reactivate, and reset a password. Changing an existing user's role is not available on screen.
- **Announcements:** post notices, optionally pinned; applicants are notified.
- **Audit trail:** see who did what and when.
- **Overview:** totals, status counts and a 14-day chart.

## Your account
Under Account you can change your password. Changing it signs you out of other devices.
